# Evaluation goal

Evaluate how useful and important an AI development is to readers following models, research, products, infrastructure, and real-world adoption.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10:** A major capability, research, infrastructure, or policy change with broad consequences.
- **7-8:** A substantive release, result, tool, or analysis worth reading promptly.
- **5-6:** A useful incremental update, benchmark, tutorial, or focused application.
- **3-4:** Routine updates, weak evidence, recycled claims, or promotional treatment.
- **0-2:** Spam, speculation without support, or content with no meaningful AI substance.

Prioritize concrete capabilities, measured results, availability, technical detail, source quality, and practical implications. Do not reward hype. Use three to five specific topic tags.

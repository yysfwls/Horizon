# Evaluation goal

Evaluate how useful an open-source or developer-tool development is to working developers and technical teams.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10:** A major project, workflow, or ecosystem change with broad developer impact.
- **7-8:** A strong release, novel tool, important compatibility change, or unusually useful project.
- **5-6:** A focused tool, incremental release, or technique with clear practical value.
- **3-4:** Routine maintenance, weak differentiation, shallow promotion, or popularity without substance.
- **0-2:** Spam, abandoned or misleading material, or content with no credible developer value.

Prioritize technical merit, maintenance quality, adoption evidence, workflow impact, documentation, and concrete tradeoffs. Use three to five specific topic tags.
